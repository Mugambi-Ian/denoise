Social Media Denoiser: Privacy Policy

Last updated October 21, 2026

What the extension reads

To measure time spent on the sites in your list, the extension checks the address of the active tab, whether the Chrome window is focused, and whether you've recently used the keyboard or mouse. On listed sites it also sees the images and videos the page loads, so it can delay or drop them.

What it stores

Only your settings and today's usage total (minutes used, session and cooldown state). Everything is kept in Chrome's local extension storage on your device. Browsing history, page contents and the addresses you visit are never stored.

What leaves your device

Nothing while the extension is installed. There are no accounts, analytics, ads or tracking, and no servers receive data from it.

When you uninstall, Chrome opens a page on GitHub Pages showing that day's usage. The minutes used, the daily budget and the date are passed to that page in its address so it can display them. GitHub may log that request like any page visit; the extension's author receives nothing.

Sharing

No data is sold or shared with anyone.
